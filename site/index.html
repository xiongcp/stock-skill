让 AI 助手用真实行情和确定性算法读 K 线 Let your AI agent read K-line charts with real data and deterministic math

kline-structure-chart 是给 Claude Code、Codex 等 AI 助手用的技能。一句话提问，助手运行 Python 在真实行情上计算 K 线结构、三重屏障风控计划、15 日正常波动范围和无前视回测，产出 JSON、PNG 结构图和离线交互看板。 kline-structure-chart is a skill for AI agents such as Claude Code and Codex. Ask one question and the agent runs Python on real market data to compute K-line structure, a triple-barrier risk plan, a 15-day normal volatility range and a no-lookahead backtest, delivered as JSON, a PNG chart and an offline interactive dashboard.

为什么不让大模型直接看图Why not let an LLM read the chart directly

看截图问股有四个老问题。这个技能的做法是：计算交给 Python，助手只转述算出来的数字。 Asking a model about a chart screenshot has four familiar problems. This skill hands the computation to Python; the agent only relays the computed numbers.

常见问题Problem多模态模型看图读错坐标轴，编造不存在的价格，画错趋势线。Vision models misread axes, invent prices and draw the wrong trend lines.
这里的做法Here所有点位都由 Python 从真实 OHLCV 序列计算一次并写入 JSON，PNG 和 HTML 只读这份结果。Every level is computed once in Python from real OHLCV data and written to JSON; the PNG and HTML only read that result.
常见问题Problem拐点和形态事后用未来 K 线重绘，回测看似完美，实盘无法复现。Swing points and patterns are redrawn with future bars; the backtest looks perfect and cannot be reproduced.
这里的做法Here单边因果平滑，每个拐点记录确认日；回测在每个决策日只用当日及之前的数据重跑线上同一条流水线。One-sided causal smoothing and a confirmation day for every swing point; the backtest reruns the live pipeline on each decision day with only the data available then.
常见问题Problem把几个指标的加权分包装成"上涨概率"，没有样本支撑。A weighted sum of indicators is presented as a "probability of going up", with no samples behind it.
这里的做法Here不输出上涨概率，规则分不叫胜率。唯一出现的胜率是回测成交胜率，附 Wilson 95% 区间和样本量。No probability of going up, and the rule score is never called a win rate. The only win rate shown is the backtest fill win rate, with a Wilson 95% interval and the sample size.
常见问题Problem大盘在跌还推荐追高；忽略 T+1、涨跌停、港股价位表和印花税等交易制度。Chasing breakouts while the market falls; ignoring T+1, price limits, Hong Kong tick tables and stamp duty.
这里的做法Here先大盘后个股：大盘环境评分低于 35 时风控一票否决做多。三市场交易制度按带生效日期和来源的规则实现。Market first: a regime score below 35 vetoes long setups. Each market's trading rules are implemented with effective dates and sources.

它能做什么What it does

CN · HK · US

三市场覆盖Three markets

A 股（沪深北个股、ETF、指数）、港股、美股，自动配对大盘基准（沪深300 / 恒生指数 / SPY）。A-shares (stocks, ETFs and indices), Hong Kong and US, each paired with its benchmark (CSI 300 / Hang Seng / SPY).

structure

K 线数学结构K-line structure

因果拐点、轨道拟合、ABC 浪型与推动 5 浪、8 类标准情景、蜡烛与双底双顶、RSI 背离、SMC、成交分布。Causal swing points, channel fitting, ABC and 5-wave counts, 8 standard scenarios, candlestick and double top/bottom patterns, RSI divergence, SMC and a volume profile.

risk plan

三重屏障风控计划Triple-barrier risk plan

入场区、止损、TP1/TP2、移动保本与持仓防守；给了账户净值就按单笔风险比例算股数。Entry zone, stop, TP1/TP2, move-to-breakeven and position defense; with an account size it computes shares from the per-trade risk fraction.

range

15 日正常波动范围15-day normal volatility range

以最新收盘价为中心的 50% / 80% 区间，只描述波动幅度、不判断方向，附滚动样本外覆盖率与折算有效样本量。50% / 80% bands centered on the latest close. They describe the size of moves, not direction, with rolling out-of-sample coverage and effective sample size.

backtest

无前视回测No-lookahead backtest

逐日回放线上计划，不学习任何参数；样本少于 30 笔时报告会明确提示参考价值低。Replays the live plan day by day without fitting any parameters; with fewer than 30 fills the report says the statistics carry little weight.

outputs

同源产物与回复守门One computation, checked replies

结果 JSON、1920×1160 结构图、离线交互看板和中文报告出自同一份计算；lint_response.py 拦截编造的价格和概率话术。Result JSON, a 1920×1160 chart, an offline dashboard and a Chinese report all come from one computation; lint_response.py blocks invented prices and probability claims.

它不做什么What it does not do

不预测涨跌No up/down predictions

不输出上涨概率；规则分（0~100）是技术条件的加权打分，不是胜率；15 日区间只描述波动幅度，不是目标价。No probability of going up; the rule score (0–100) is a weighted score of technical conditions, not a win rate; the 15-day range describes the size of moves and is not a price target.

不造数据No fabricated data

只用新浪、Yahoo、腾讯的真实行情或你提供的本地真实文件；取不到就以 [DATA_UNAVAILABLE] 退出，不估算、不补数。Only real data from Sina, Yahoo and Tencent, or real local files you provide; if data is unavailable it exits with [DATA_UNAVAILABLE] instead of estimating.

不替你下单No order placement

只做规则化技术分析与研究验证，不连接券商、不执行交易。Rule-based technical analysis and research validation only; it does not connect to brokers or execute trades.

不做基本面No fundamentals

基本面、新闻、财报都不在范围内；成交分布是 OHLCV 近似，不等于真实持仓。Fundamentals, news and earnings are out of scope; the volume profile approximates traded volume from OHLCV and is not actual holdings.

本技能输出的所有内容都是基于历史行情的规则化技术分析，仅供研究与学习参考，不构成任何投资建议。市场有风险，决策需独立判断。 Everything this skill outputs is rule-based technical analysis of historical market data, for research and learning only. It is not investment advice. Markets carry risk; make your own decisions.
